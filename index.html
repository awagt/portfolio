Tools I built

Where off-the-shelf systems left a gap, I designed and shipped lightweight web apps on Google Apps Script, Google Sheets and Firebase. Each one captures operational data at source and turns it into reporting.

Last-mile & internal consolidation tracker

SLA · TIMING

Tracks how long each order spends in every stage, from store-to-hub consolidation through courier hand-over to delivery. Every scan writes a timestamp, so the app shows dwell time per stage, flags orders that breach SLA, and pinpoints whether a delay sits with a store, the hub or a 3PL.

Stack
Web app · barcode scanning · Google Apps Script · Google Sheets
Output
Stage-by-stage turnaround, SLA breaches and partner performance

Stock Transfer Order scan tool

WAREHOUSE

Browser-based barcode scanner for inter-store and warehouse transfers. Validates every scan against the STO in real time and writes it back to the sheet per scan, so progress survives a page refresh.

Controls
Over-scan, wrong-STO and cross-transfer alerts, with audio feedback
Stack
HTML/JS · Apps Script web app (doGet/doPost) · Google Sheets

Dispatch & returns trackers
